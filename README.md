##My Wallet - A Simple Expense and Receipt Tracker
##My Wallet is an easy-to-use Android app designed to help you manage your finances efficiently. With My Wallet, you can effortlessly keep track of your money by recording every expense and receipt, complete with descriptions and amounts. The app represents your financial transactions in a simple and clear way, making it a valuable tool for maintaining financial clarity.

#Features
My Wallet offers the following key features:

Record Financial Operations

Input the amount and description of your financial operations.
The app stores this information in a singly linked list for easy access and management.
Visual Representation

My Wallet displays the recorded financial operations in a clear and intuitive manner using a RecyclerView.
Each entry includes the amount, description, and date-time of the financial operation.
Budget Calculation

The app calculates your budget by counting the total number of financial operations in the list.
It then presents this budget in a straightforward manner.
Data Persistence

My Wallet stores and retrieves your financial data using shared preferences.
This feature allows you to save and retrieve data using key-value pairs.
Your data is securely stored locally on your device.
